FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How are predictions delivered?

Predictions are delivered directly through your subscription dashboard. Higher tiers get priority delivery and early access before events begin.

What does "self-annealing" mean?

Our models continuously recalibrate themselves using the latest match data, improving accuracy over time without manual intervention — the system learns and adapts automatically.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. All subscriptions are month-to-month with no contracts. Cancel from your dashboard at any time — you'll retain access through the end of your billing period.

What sports are you adding next?

NBA and WNBA models are actively in development. Darts and table tennis are on the roadmap for 2026. Subscribe to stay updated on new engine launches.

How is this different from tipsters?

We don't give "picks" or "locks." We deliver calibrated probabilities from backtested machine-learning models. You make the decisions — we give you the math.

Do you have a track record?

All models are walk-forward backtested on historical data before going live. We believe in transparency and publish model performance metrics for subscribers.
